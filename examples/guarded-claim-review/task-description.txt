- gate: required capability claim.review.gate in pool validation; activation is

The review_policy input must have exactly schema claim-review-policy-v1,
max_revisions 1, and five distinct actors: claim-assessor, claim-reviser,
claim-gate, claim-semantic, and claim-acceptor. No actor may perform another
stage. The root acceptance policy is independent, versioned
claim-review-closure-v1, requires claim.review.accept, and names the three base
Asset IDs as evidence_asset_ids when the operator creates the root task.

The report is a JSON object with exactly claims, revised_summary, and
limitations. Each claim record contains claim_id, original_text, verdict,
evidence_excerpt_ids, rationale, revised_text, and evidence_bindings
([{excerpt_id, quote}]). Claims preserve source IDs and original text exactly;
revised_summary and limitations are non-empty. The verdict taxonomy is:
supported means the source supports the claim at its stated strength and scope;
overstated means the source supports a narrower or weaker version, including
upgrading an observational association to a causal effect; unsupported means
the source does not support even the underlying observation or contradicts it.
Do not encode specific claim answers in this task description. Structural stages
can prove explicit binding and literal quote membership; they cannot prove that
free-form rationale is semantically persuasive. Semantic review is the separate
model-backed check.

One revision is allowed. A normal protocol retry may recover a failed stage,
but it must not consume or silently replace an already published revision.
If the gate fails, it can publish a durable failure report and leave the root
without qualification; this is an explicit blocked outcome, not an automatic
repair controller. Do not claim that the workflow guarantees semantic truth.
